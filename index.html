Data Sources & Provenance

All calculations executed by this engine rely on real demographic distributions compiled from global international institutions. Here is where the math originates:

1. World Population & Country Weights Link

Source: UN World Population Prospects. Used for nation-by-nation headcount and regional state weight calculations.

2. Age Pyramid Distributions Link

Source: U.S. Census Bureau International Database & UN Age Cohorts. Calculates realistic percentage splits between ages 18 to 80+.

3. Religious & Secular Demographics Link

Source: Pew Research Center - Global Religious Landscape Study. Provides global proportions for major world belief systems.

4. Global Employment & Professions Link

Source: International Labour Organization (ILOSTAT) & National Labor Surveys. Weighs sector employment percentages worldwide.

5. Orientation & LGBTQ+ Data Link

Source: Gallup LGBTQ+ Demographics Survey & Ipsos LGBT+ Pride Global Survey. Provides weighted identity preferences.
